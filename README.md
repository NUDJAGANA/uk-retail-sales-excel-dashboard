# Obsidian Intelligence | UK Retail Sales Dashboard

### Excel Business Intelligence Portfolio Project | 2024–2025

![UK Retail Sales Dashboard](Obsidian_Intelligence_UK_Retail_Sales_Dashboard.png)

## Project Overview

This project analyses a synthetic UK retail dataset containing 3,000 transaction records from 2024 to 2025.

The objective was to transform raw sales data into a visually engaging Excel dashboard that supports business performance monitoring and decision-making.

## Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel formulas and KPI calculations
- Data visualisation and dashboard design

## Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Net Sales | £198,174.26 |
| Total Gross Profit | £109,896.96 |
| Overall Gross Profit Margin | 55.45% |
| Highest-Revenue Region | South West |
| South West Net Sales | £26,993.95 |

## Key Business Insights

**1. Regional Sales Leadership**

South West generated the highest net sales among the identified regions, reaching £26,993.95.

**2. Close Regional Competition**

Scotland followed closely behind South West, with a revenue difference of only £61.71.

**3. Overall Profitability**

The business achieved an overall gross profit margin of approximately 55.45%, indicating that gross profit represented slightly more than half of net sales.

## Project Workflow

1. Imported and examined the raw retail dataset.
2. Cleaned and transformed data using Power Query.
3. Calculated gross sales, net sales, total costs and gross profit.
4. Developed PivotTables and PivotCharts for regional and monthly analysis.
5. Created KPI calculations and linked dashboard cards.
6. Designed the Obsidian Intelligence dashboard using a consistent dark-themed visual style.
7. Validated dashboard metrics and prepared the final visualisation.

## Dashboard Features

- Three financial KPI cards
- Regional sales comparison chart
- Monthly sales performance trend
- Business insight cards
- Consistent, presentation-ready visual design

## Dataset

The project uses synthetic retail data for educational and portfolio purposes. Results do not represent actual UK retail industry performance.
